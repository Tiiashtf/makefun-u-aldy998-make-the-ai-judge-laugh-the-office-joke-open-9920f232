> I put 'per my last email' in my out-of-office reply, so now even my vacation is passive-aggressive.

The setup quotes the office's most dreaded phrase — "per my last email" — and the payoff turns it back on itself: the out-of-office reply meant to switch work off has made the vacation itself passive-aggressive. One specific office detail, one turn, 99 characters.
